About the product

Roasted Makhana: What You're Buying

Khao Better roasted makhana starts as the seed of Euryale ferox, a water lily that has been farmed in the shallow ponds of north Bihar for generations. The seed is harvested by hand, sun-dried, and popped with dry heat until it puffs into a light white kernel. That kernel is the whole product. Nothing is extruded, moulded, coated in batter or reconstituted from flour, which is what separates makhana from most things sold as a crunchy snack.

From there, our only job is to not ruin it. Graded seed goes into a rotary drum and is dry-roasted at around 140°C until it turns brittle enough to shatter between your teeth. It is then tumbled for under a minute with cold-pressed sunflower oil and ground spices — just enough to carry flavour onto the surface — before being nitrogen-flushed and sealed the same day. At no stage is the seed submerged in oil. That is the entire reason the label reads 0 g trans fat and 0 mg cholesterol, and it is why we put "roasted, not fried" on the front of the pack rather than in the small print.

A 25 g pack — one serve — carries 113 kcal, 2.6 g of dietary fibre, 1.6 g of naturally occurring sugar, no added sugar, and 117 mg of sodium. Per 100 g that works out to 450 kcal, 10.3 g fibre and 6.1 g protein. We publish both columns on every product page because per-100 g figures are the ones you can actually compare across brands, and per-pack figures are the ones that matter when you are standing in a kitchen at 5 pm. We are not going to tell you makhana is a weight-loss product or that it treats anything: it is a dry-roasted snack with a label we are happy to have read out loud.

Buying roasted makhana online usually means guessing at seed grade. Ours is 5+ suta — the large, uniform, fully-popped grade — hand-graded before roasting, because small or partly-popped seed goes chewy in the pouch within weeks. We buy it directly from 240 farming families in Darbhanga and Madhubani at a fixed floor price, paid within seven days, with no commission agent in between. Six flavours are in development: Classic Roasted is on sale now at ₹49 for a 25 g pack, and Peri Peri, Pudina Punch, Achari Chatpata, Tangy Tomato and Cheese & Herb are in final roast trials.
